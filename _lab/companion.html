Panel 01

Load & greet

On load the robot parks at the LEFT edge, faces the viewer and plays Wave once, then cross-fades to Idle. Reload to watch it again.

Panel 02

One panel, one crossing

Each panel gets a complete edge-to-edge walk. This one runs right → left, because direction alternates with the panel index — so panels join end to end and he never teleports.

Panel 03

Stop scrolling

Stop for a moment: after the ~150ms debounce he cross-fades back to Idle and stops moving. The idle loop renders at half rate to save GPU.

Panel 04

Scroll back up

The target is a pure function of scroll position, so scrolling up retraces the route exactly — no drift, no accumulated error.

Panel 05

Edge turnaround

He arrives at the edge exactly as this panel hands over to the next, plays Jump once, bounces inward and turns 180° to start the next crossing.
