01

Load & greet

On load he parks at the LEFT edge, faces the viewer and plays Wave once, then settles into Idle. Reload to watch it again.

02

He wanders on his own

Scrolling does nothing to him. He picks a random spot along the bottom edge, turns, and walks or runs there depending on how far it is.

03

Arrive and think

On arrival he squares up to camera and plays a thinking beat — a nod or a head shake — then stands about for a few seconds before choosing somewhere new.

04

Always facing you

Watch the faces viewer readout: it reads yes for every idle, wave and thinking frame. Only walking and running turn him side-on.

05

Bounds

Destinations are clamped to his silhouette, not his origin, so he never crosses the edge of the screen however the dice land.
