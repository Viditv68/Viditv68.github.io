Titles on store shelves

Console and mobile titles I've worked on as a developer — gameplay, progression systems and the tools behind them.

Published by Sony

Lokko

Appy Monkeys · Unity Developer · PS5 · BitSummit & GDC

A Unity title published by Sony. I built the two-player co-op robot level end to end — local two-controller couch co-op on a single console, both players riding one mech: a pilot who drives and punches, and a gunner who fires blocks.

  • Designed the gunner's arc-and-crosshair preview, so blocks snap to each other into runnable platforms that block obstacles, plus punchable cube-spheres that shatter into scattering cubes.
  • Architected a custom level editor scene letting players build levels with real-time preview and validation.
  • Implemented the full iOS control scheme across every gameplay mechanic for responsive touch input.
  • Designed a wave-based enemy spawner with multiple enemy types and difficulty scaling for the Lab levels.
  • Engineered secure auth on AWS Cognito — email OTP verification and account deletion via Amazon SES — and shipped the in-game wallet.
